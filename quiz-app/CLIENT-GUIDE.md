# The Quiz Master Challenge — User Guide

A simple guide to running and managing your quiz game. No technical knowledge needed.

---

## 1. What you have

A neon, game-show style quiz app with **two sides**:

- **Game panel** — where you host a live quiz for players/teams.
- **Admin panel** — where you create and manage the quizzes and questions.

Everything runs in a normal web browser. Nothing to install.

## 3. Managing your quizzes (Admin panel)

1. Open the website and go to the **Admin** login.
2. Sign in with the admin username and password above.
3. From here you can:
   - **Create a new quiz** — give it a name/subject and add questions and answers.
   - **View / edit quizzes** — change questions, fix typos, or delete a quiz.
   - **Add media** — attach an image or video to a question to make it richer.

Your changes save automatically and are ready to use the next time you host a game.

---

## 4. Hosting a live game (Game panel)

1. Open the website and go to the **Game** login.
2. Sign in with the same admin account.
3. Pick a quiz, set up your teams, and start playing.
4. During play: questions are revealed one at a time, answers lock in, and the
   scoreboard updates as you go.
5. At the end, the final scores and winner are shown.

---

## 5. Where your data lives

All quizzes, questions, and game results are stored safely in an online database.
You don't need to back anything up manually — it's saved automatically as you work.
